# Bíblia Livre

- Identity: corpus `biblia-livre`; unresolved edition `biblia-livre-unresolved`; package
  `pkg-biblia-livre-unresolved`.
- Source: official repository `https://github.com/blivre/BibliaLivre`; no concrete artifact was
  selected.
- Version: unresolved. The project says the edition date must accompany attribution.
- Rights: official project metadata declares CC BY 3.0 Brasil, while a trusted distributor exposes
  CC BY 4.0 metadata for `porbr2018`.
- Attribution: Diego Santos, Mario Sérgio and Marco Teles; exact required text must come from the
  selected edition/artifact and must not be rewritten.
- Status: `conflicting-metadata`; automatic redistribution and bundling are blocked.
- Known issues: edition (`tr`, `n4` or distributor package), revision and artifact are not yet
  fixed; license version differs across sources.
- Decision: preserve both declarations. Do not choose a license until the first importer targets a
  concrete edition and its accompanying rights notice.

Evidence consulted on 2026-08-26:

- `https://github.com/blivre/BibliaLivre/blob/master/README.md` (official project, CC BY 3.0 BR)
- `https://ebible.org/details.php?id=porbr2018` (trusted distributor, CC BY 4.0 metadata)
